Library Information, Guidelines & Services

Everything you need to know about borrowing privileges, study facilities, and digital access.

🕒

Operating Hours

  • Monday – Friday: 8:00 AM – 10:00 PM
  • Saturday – Sunday: 9:00 AM – 8:00 PM
  • Digital Catalog: Available 24/7 online
  • Holiday Closures: Notified 2 weeks in advance via portal
📖

Borrowing & Returns

  • Student Quota: Up to 5 books concurrently
  • Loan Duration: 14 standard calendar days
  • Renewals: 1 renewal permitted if no waitlist exists
  • Instant Returns: Drop off in book bin or return via account
💵

Overdue & Lost Book Policies

  • Grace Period: 24 hours following the due date
  • Late Fee: $0.50 per day per overdue book
  • Account Hold: Applied if balance exceeds $10.00
  • Damaged / Lost: Replacement cost plus $15 processing fee
🏛️

Spaces & Technology

  • Quiet Study Pods: Floors 2 and 3
  • Group Collaboration Rooms: Floor 1 (Reservation required)
  • High-Speed Wi-Fi: Network Library-Scholar
  • Duplex Printing & Scanners: Ground floor commons
